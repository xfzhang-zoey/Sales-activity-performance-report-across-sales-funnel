
**Data assets are composed of 4 data sets**: 
1. CRM SAP C4C Accounts info: sales person, customer Accounts under scope, customer accounts'marketing attributes (KARE prioritization, KARE category, Customer ABC group, Vertical market, geo locatin)
2. CRM SAP C4C Sales man activity log: registered sales activities (customer meeting, 
3. CRM SAP C4C Opportunity: customer Accounts and related sales Opportunities
4. Sales (reported via reseller portal Point of Sales + direct sales via SAP Accounting) data set: concluded Opportunities (Opportunity ID/Quote ID), SKU, Qty, Revenue, Bill-to account

**Methods**: 

SNOWFLAKE: left join, full join, inner join; Customer SQL; dataset preview, dataset publishing; 

TABLEAU SOFTWARE: Coherence control (nb of records) for data sets from Snowflake; Data Viz automation.
